I will be talking about how I am planning to wrangle my brain with habits. I haven't read any books about this, cookie cutter and box habit recipes don't really work well for me in the long term (I have tried). 
  

## Methods 

I have create a world were I can just coast and this is something that I have struggled with my entire life. I need to create a system of living that has routine friction that will allow me to struggle (just a little bit).

I have lived in a world where I can avoid, dodge and remove struggle. My brain and body doesn't like it in the short term. When it comes to the long term these dodges slowly poison me until I get sick.

I know I should confront these problems when I see them but I have no mechanism to force me to. My reliance on social and financial force to get me to do anything is toxic to my health as well. What is a guy to do? I cant ignore my issues and solving them at the last minute ruins me. 

Motivational Force Types:
- Social
	- Guilt
	- Expectations
- Financial
	- Out of money
	- Needing a Deal

I hear the world shout the simple solution "do the fucking work!"

I'm telling you, it doesn't work in the long term. I have the power to do it for a week or two, maximum a month and then I am back to old habits. 

This reveals the issue I have, the solution isn't found in the action layer. It is found in the habitual layer. I rely on habits that create foundations of actions. 

There are things that my mind craves for a habit to take root.
These are the Motivators that drive my mind.


## P.U.N.C
**Passion, Urgency, Novelty, Challenge

The habits I have cultivated that have stuck around.
### Walk the dog
 - P - I love my dog and want to see him happy
 - U - My dog puts a palpable social pressure on me and he needs to pee and eat 
 - N - I listen to podcasts, I like to see the weather/moon, knowing whats going on around
 - C - Getting outside is hard and a lot of the time I would rather not do it

This was developed by force. At the apex of self advocacy my dog stands as a proud lord as I am peon eating questionable leftovers. He performs open heart sergey on me daily pulling at my heartstrings with the skill of a creature with thumbs. Over that time I have really valued the routine of being able to tag along on his adventures.

### Daily Planner
- P - I love drawing and taking notes
- U - I have to do it in the morning otherwise it wont work
- N - I iterate and create within it and using it in different ways is always fun
- C - Doing something this consistently is crazy for me and I love looking back to see how many I have done

The daily planner is an outlier for my habits. As it is one of the first that isn't driven by any of the forces. It was carried by the motivators of Novelty and Challenge slowly revealing it's utility in my life. 

### Time Sensitive
I am time sensitive, on reflection. If a task is 15 min or less I will do it.
Like crash dieting I will find motivation and lock in and say "I can do this thing for an hour everyday!". Resulting in me not wanting to do it at all.. I recognise this is a totally unique experience and not one other person in the world has ever come to this conclusion but I just need to be brave and say my truth~.
 
## Goal

I have to use this self reflections to create habits that integrate:
- Friction
- Force
- Motivation


What I want to create is a way to boost my professional ability. 
I have been mired with imposer syndrome and which has been sapping my motivation. 
I have had a few ideas but I have found somethings that will help me.
 - 1 - They system has to be completely mine
	 - If I copy anything, use an LLM or get help from a person get it done quickly but I loose motivation
- 2 - I need to clear the runway
	- While I am starting out any stumbling block spells doom for me
	- Create an easy process to achieve
- 3 - Have a what a full bite looks like
	- What is the minimum viable actions to say this is complete?
	- Any extra flair is a bonus
	- eg: 
		- walk dog 
			- Walk around until he pees
			- Eats dinner
			-  Is wiped down
		- Daily planner
			- Date
			- Cover Art
			- Planner points
			- What is coming up in the day
- 4 - To what end?
	- Why am I doing this? (overall)
	- Dog, happiness for the boy
	- Planner, allows me to see the passage of time
