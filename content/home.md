I am doing by best to show my profesional growth through this site.

There is a part of me that is hesitent to display myself. The eyes of others has always been a menacing temptation.

I hope to make this little personalised hamlet of creativity and learning so that I can safely explore being seen. Not for my charming personality that I often lean on, but for having a depth of knowledge.

## Why me?

Throughout my life I have been able to coast. Through struggling. I know that sounds weird, but the effort I put in is a direct result of the siutation.

Why stand in the river bed at all? The struggle for nurturing mativated action toward my goals. The technique of taking gentle steps towards the bank of this river is nearly lost as the focus on swimming in crisis has been my only transport.
